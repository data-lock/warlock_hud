# Warlock HUD v0.29.2

This release adds lightweight Soulstone and Healthstone tools for World of
Warcraft: Forever beta (interface 16001).

## What's new

- A self-applied Soulstone shows the recipient beneath the Soulstone icon while
  its aura is confirmed active. The Stones page offers recipient and estimated
  countdown options. The label clears when the aura is lost.
- The Stones page records group members who received a Healthstone in a
  completed trade. `/whub stones` lists those players; the Stones page can
  start a new distribution round. A supplied entry records the trade, not
  current Healthstone possession.
- Optional automatic placement adds one Healthstone to an empty trade with a
  current group member. It leaves occupied trades and non-group traders alone.
  You must press **Accept** yourself.
- Healthstone trade notices and distribution records wait for the client's
  trade-complete message; cancelled trades are not recorded.
- Warlock HUD is enabled by default for Warlocks and disabled by default for
  other classes. Each character can change this on the General page.
- `/whub trace` opens a copyable diagnostic window with separate Trade and
  Soulstone recording controls.

## Soulstone limits

Soulstone use after death cannot be identified reliably from the observed
Forever events. A stone lost around death is marked `LOST / UNKNOWN`, never
`USED`. Countdown and expiry information depend on safe aura observations and
can be stale during combat. Expiry, reload recovery, and the Soulstone group
announcement still need in-game verification.

The existing native aura tracker and Blizzard cooldown rendering remain in
place.

## Install

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.29.2.zip` from this release and extract it into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. The ZIP contains the
`Warlock_Hud` folder. Use `/reload` after updating.
