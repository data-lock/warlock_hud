# Warlock HUD v0.30.0

This release adds Soul Shard warnings and a temporary on-screen demo of every
HUD bar for World of Warcraft: Forever beta (interface 16001).

## What's new

- The Soul Shard icon flashes red at 4 shards or fewer and yellow at 18 or
  more by default. An optional yellow warning also detects shards in regular
  bags while a Soul Bag is equipped. Warning thresholds are per profile and
  independent of the minimum kept when deleting shards.
- The Soul Shards page uses sliders for its reserve and warning thresholds.
  The icon visibility checkbox can be turned back on after being unchecked.
- **Demo all HUD bars** on the General page temporarily shows every icon slot at its
  on-screen position, including disabled slots and inactive buffs and procs.
  The bars are labeled. **End HUD demo** closes the preview without changing
  live aura tracking.
- Siphon Life has its own Target DoT slot, using Blizzard's native aura and
  countdown display. The passive Soul Siphon icon has been removed from Utility.
- The Buffs row keeps its left edge fixed as visible buffs change. Existing
  saved positions are migrated to preserve that edge.
- The Profiles summary no longer overlaps the current-profile dropdown.

## Testing note

The on-screen demo, settings layout, and Buffs row position were reviewed in
game. Siphon Life's live aura countdown and Soul Bag overflow detection still
need an in-game check on Forever.

## Install

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.30.0.zip` from this release and extract it into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. The ZIP contains the
`Warlock_Hud` folder. Use `/reload` after updating.
