# Warlock HUD v0.32.11

This release adds Curse and Bane assignments for Warlocks in World of Warcraft:
Forever beta (interface 16001).

## What's new

- Open **Group Assignments** from Tracked Auras or type `/whud assignments`.
  Assign a Curse and Bane to each Warlock in your group, including players who
  do not have the addon. Solo Warlocks can assign themselves.
- Group members using Warlock HUD can edit assignments. Changes sync between
  addon users, and a player who reloads can request the current assignments.
  Personal assignments survive a reload; group assignments clear on disband.
- Your assigned spells become the idle Curse and Bane HUD images and determine
  their range checks. Active effects retain Blizzard's native icons and
  countdowns.
- A different successful Curse or Bane cast produces a local warning and a
  purple highlight on the relevant HUD slot. The window never casts spells.
- Resize the full window or switch to a compact roster view. The **Announce**
  button sends the current assignments to the displayed group channel only when
  clicked. `/whud announceassignments` does the same.

## Verification

The v0.32.11 ZIP was reviewed in game, and the maintainer reported that the
tested features worked. A copy was also shared with a guildmate for testing.

## Install

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.32.11.zip` from this release and extract it into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. The ZIP contains the
`Warlock_Hud` folder. Use `/reload` after updating.
