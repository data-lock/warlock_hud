# Warlock HUD v0.34.0

This release adds spell-rank reminders and pet grimoire tracking for World of
Warcraft: Forever beta (interface 16001).

## What's new

- Get a chat warning when a direct spell button on your action bar uses an
  older rank than the highest rank you know. The addon also warns after every
  successful lower-rank cast, including casts from macros and the spellbook.
- The addon remembers each summoned demon's spell ranks for your character.
  Summon each demon once to build its initial record.
- At demon trainers, grimoire icon outlines show the status: **red** for a
  rank already known, **green** for an unlearned rank you can buy, **yellow**
  when a higher usable rank of that spell is also for sale, and **grey** when
  the pet has not been scanned and you meet the item's level requirement.
  Tooltips explain known, unscanned, and better-rank status. The indicators
  follow the vendor's pages.

## Install

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.34.0.zip` and extract it into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. The ZIP contains the
`Warlock_Hud` folder. Use `/reload` after updating.
