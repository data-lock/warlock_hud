# Warlock HUD v0.36.2

This update adds Conflagrate tracking to the Utility row in World of Warcraft:
Forever beta (interface 16001).

- Conflagrate appears when learned and shows its Blizzard-native spell
  cooldown. Its slot can be hidden or reordered in the current profile.
- The icon is grey until the existing native Immolate tracker shows your
  Immolate on the current target. It then turns colored and pulses, returning
  to grey when Immolate expires, is consumed, or the target changes.
- The Conflagrate cue uses the native target aura widget; it does not scan
  target auras in Lua during combat.

The Conflagrate cue was verified in game after `/reload`.

## Install

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.36.2.zip` and extract it into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. The ZIP contains the
`Warlock_Hud` folder. Use `/reload` after updating.
