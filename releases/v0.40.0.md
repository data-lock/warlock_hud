# Warlock HUD v0.40.0

This release adds an optional Multi-Target DoT tracker for World of Warcraft:
Forever beta (interface 16001).

- See up to 16 relevant enemies in a compact, scrollable window. Rows show
  portraits, names, health bars, raid markers when available, a 20% health
  mark, and Blizzard-native DoT icons and countdowns.
- Discover enemies through visible nameplates and player, party, or raid
  target tokens when group combat involvement or one of your DoTs can be
  confirmed. Dead enemies leave the list and remaining rows move up.
- Keep the tracker disabled by default. Use `/whud mtd` outside combat to
  open or close it. Adjust its position, size, transparency, and optional
  combat-only visibility in settings.
- Keep the tracker view-only. Select enemies through world nameplates or Tab.
  Toggling, moving, and resizing require leaving combat. Enemies without an
  exposed unit token cannot be shown.

The tracker was tested in game during solo and party combat. Raid-specific
behavior has not yet been tested in a raid.

## Install

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.40.0.zip` and extract it into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. The ZIP contains the
`Warlock_Hud` folder. Use `/reload` after updating.
