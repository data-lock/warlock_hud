# Warlock HUD v0.42.22 — Multi-Target DoT Damage Estimates

The optional Multi-Target DoTs window now shows a colored estimate of the
remaining damage from Corruption, Bane of Agony, Siphon Life, and Immolate.
Each spell has its own color, and the line uses the full health-bar width as
its scale. It appears when the cast succeeds, then can adjust from observed
noncritical ticks. Estimates are approximate; Immolate's direct hit is not
included.

- The damage line is thicker, the 20% marker draws above it, and health text
  sits above the bar. Health color shifts from green toward red as it falls.
- The Demonic Brand target icon uses Searing Pain's range. The active native
  aura texture remains under Blizzard's control.
- Active proc icons use Blizzard's native group layout to center the visible
  set on the saved proc anchor.
- A portrait flash for estimated lethal DoT damage was tried and removed
  because protected combat health prevented it from working. It remains on
  the future-work list.

The Corruption and Bane damage line was checked in game on two mobs during
review. The final proc centering, health color gradient, Siphon Life and
Immolate estimates, and Searing Pain range behavior need an in-game check
after maintenance. Source syntax and smoke tests passed.

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.42.22.zip` and extract its `Warlock_Hud` folder into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. Use `/reload` after
updating.
