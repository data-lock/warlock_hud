# Warlock HUD v0.43.0 — Multi-Target DoT Awareness

The optional Multi-Target DoTs window now makes each mob's health and your
remaining DoT damage easier to compare. Each live row shows current HP, health
percent, and an approximate remaining DoT total. Colored segments for
Corruption, Bane of Agony, Siphon Life, and Immolate use the full health bar as
their scale. The health bar shifts toward red as a mob nears death, and its
portrait pulses red when the estimated DoT damage exceeds current health.

When a mob's nameplate disappears, the tracker can keep a grey **last-seen**
row while you remain in combat and its estimated DoTs are active. The row
retains the last available HP, health-bar fill, portrait when readable, and
estimated active DoT icons. It returns to a live row if the mob becomes
available through a nameplate or another tracked unit token. Last-seen rows
clear when combat ends or the estimated DoTs expire. A reported evade also
clears the estimate.

The 20% health marker is shorter and narrower. HP and DoT numbers use compact
labels such as `9.00k` and `1.00M` when the values are readable. The row layout
also gives the right-aligned DoT icons and HP label more room.

## Limitations

- Damage is an estimate, not a kill guarantee. It favors readable spell-rank
  descriptions, includes a best-effort Curse of the Elements modifier, and can
  use observed noncritical ticks when a spell total is unavailable. Critical
  hits, resists, other damage modifiers, early aura removal, and Immolate's
  direct hit can make the result differ from actual remaining damage.
- A grey row's HP, health bar, and portrait are **last-seen snapshots**. They
  do not update while the mob has no live unit token. Its DoT number continues
  to decrease by the cast-time estimate, not by reading an absent aura.
- The client may not provide a readable HP or portrait snapshot. In that case
  the row shows its DoT estimate and a generic portrait instead. Raid markers
  are not saved on grey rows.
- Grey rows have no native aura countdowns or lethal portrait pulse. These
  resume only when the client exposes the mob again through a tracked token.
- A last-seen row requires a readable mob GUID before its token disappears.
  The client can restrict GUID access, so some mobs may still drop from the
  window. The window shows at most 16 live and last-seen mobs combined.
- An evade clears the estimate only when the client reports it for a tracked
  unit. Otherwise the row remains until combat ends or its estimate expires.

The Multi-Target window is enabled by default for new profiles and remains
view-only. Existing profiles that turned it off stay off. Toggle it outside
combat with `/whud mtd`; target enemies with world nameplates or Tab. Window
movement, resizing, and toggling remain unavailable during combat.

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.43.0.zip` and extract its `Warlock_Hud` folder into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. Use `/reload` after
updating.
