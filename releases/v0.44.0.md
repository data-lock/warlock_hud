# Warlock HUD v0.44.0 — Multi-Target Threat Awareness

Each live enemy in the Multi-Target DoTs window can now show your personal
threat at a glance: a green dot and **LOW**, a yellow dot and **MED**, or a red
dot and **HIGH**. A grey dash means threat is unknown or unavailable. These
labels are categories, not percentages or a ranking against other players.

The indicator is on by default. Turn it off under **Multi-Target DoTs → Show
Threat Indicator**. The choice is saved per profile, included in share codes,
and resets to on with default settings. Disabling it gives enemy names their
full available width again. The existing health bars, DoT damage estimates,
portraits, HP labels, 20% marker, and native DoT countdowns remain in place.

Grey last-seen rows always show unknown threat. When the client confirms that
a mob died, its last-seen row and remaining DoT estimate clear promptly.
Enemies that only move out of view retain the existing last-seen behavior.

## Forever API notes

In solo combat testing, Forever returned readable personal threat statuses
for `target` and nameplate tokens and fired threat update events. The addon
checks for restricted values before interpreting the result. A failed,
restricted, or absent result displays the grey dash. Party and raid target
token behavior, group combat with four or more enemies, and the status `2`
still need in-game verification. The indicator never infers a threat
percentage from another signal.

Download `Warlock_Hud-v0.44.0.zip` and extract its `Warlock_Hud` folder into
`World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/`. Use `/reload` after
updating.
