# Warlock HUD v1.0.1

## Proc tracking

The configurable proc row now includes Decimation. It appears while the active
10-second player buff is present and uses the client's native aura countdown.

## Rows & Layout

Target DoT and Utility icons can again be reordered within their grids and moved
between the two grids by dragging the gold grips. Their Edit Mode movers also
remain visible when the rows have no currently available icons.
