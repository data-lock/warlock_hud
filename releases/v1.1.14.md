# Warlock HUD v1.1.14

- Give your pet frame a flashing low-health alert at 35% health or below. Choose its color, flash speed, and opacity range, or turn it off under **Pet Health Alert**.
- Add WoW: Forever's Wrack to castbar channel tick markers.

Because forgetting to heal your demon and watching it clock out mid-fight is a rite of passage, the alert is customizable and hard to miss.

After updating, use `/reload`. Check the **Pet Health Alert** options page and verify the alert with your pet below 35% health. Channel tick markers should also appear while channeling Wrack.
