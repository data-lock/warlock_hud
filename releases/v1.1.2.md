# Warlock HUD v1.1.2

- Add configurable cooldown tracking for Torment, Suffering, Phase Shift, Lash
  of Pain, Soothing Kiss, Devour Magic, and Spell Lock. Each icon appears when
  the active pet knows that ability and uses its learned rank.
- Fix duplicate MTD rows caused when combat restricts a live unit's GUID while
  its last-seen DoT estimate remains active.
- Retry demon trainer spellbook scans when the trainer first opens, merge
  Incubus and Succubus grimoire records, and tint known grimoire icons.

After updating, use `/reload` and check the pet cooldown Utility row, MTD rows
in a dungeon, and the demon trainer grimoire indicators in game.
