# Warlock HUD v1.1.4

- Improve demon trainer grimoire detection for ranked and rankless books,
  including Phase Shift. Tooltips report whether the relevant demon already
  knows the spell or still needs to learn it.
- Mark books above your character's level in red, already known books in red,
  available missing books in green, higher available ranks in yellow, and
  eligible books for unscanned demons in grey.
- Keep grimoire marks after buying a book and reduce redundant merchant-page
  work. Pet spellbooks are scanned on pet or spellbook updates.
- Add a **Grimoire Log** toggle to `/whud debug` and `/whud grimdebug` for
  merchant row, availability, level, and pet-scan diagnostics.

After updating, use `/reload`. Check a demon trainer for known, missing,
rankless, and above-level grimoires. Buying a grimoire should leave known-book
marks visible after the merchant window updates.
