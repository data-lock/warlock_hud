# Warlock HUD v1.1.8

- Add Rain of Fire tick markers to the native player castbar, spaced at its four
  two-second ticks. Existing Drain Life, Drain Mana, and Drain Soul markers stay
  in place. Turn channel tick markers off from General → Spell Feedback.
- Add a General setting to disable lower-rank spell warnings from action bars
  and successful casts.
- Add Death Coil and Shadow Ward to the configurable Utility cooldown row when
  those spells are learned.
- Show Blizzard-native countdown numbers on cooldown icons, using whole seconds
  and minute:second formatting without millisecond decimals.
- Add a 35% health marker alongside the existing 20% marker. Each marker has
  its own setting under Rows & Layout → Nameplates. Both apply to attackable
  nameplates, the target health bar, and Multi-Target health bars.
- Clarify that Soul Shard deletion requires a deliberate icon click, removes one
  regular-bag shard at a time, and stops at the configured reserve.

After updating, use `/reload`. Check Rain of Fire and Drain castbar markers, the
two new cooldown icons, cooldown number readability, and each health marker's
independent setting.
