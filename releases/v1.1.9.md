# Warlock HUD v1.1.9

- Add a General → Cooldown Display setting to show abilities only while they
  are on cooldown. It covers Warlock spells, pet abilities, racials, and Fear
  and Banish trackers; ready icons are hidden when the client reports them ready.
- Fix errors in cooldown icon visibility updates and the settings toggle callback.
- Coalesce Multi-Target health, aura, target, threat, and nameplate event refreshes
  into 150 ms batches, reducing repeated identity, row layout, and damage work
  during event bursts. Nameplate identity clearing and native aura refreshes
  remain immediate.

After updating, use `/reload`. Check the cooldown setting with ready and active
spells, pet abilities, and a racial. Confirm the Multi-Target display still
updates after health, aura, target, and nameplate changes.
